About Zeecon SL Ltd

Zeecon SL Ltd is a registered construction company based in Freetown, Sierra Leone. We work across real estate, residential projects, wash facilities, and renovations.

We take pride in effective service delivery and building strong relationships with our clients. To help every project stand the test of time, we source quality finishing and construction materials directly from factories locally and internationally.
